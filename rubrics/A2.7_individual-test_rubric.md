## Grading Notes

- **All parts are summative** (`summative: true`). The grading brief and every rubric
  description stay off the page, and submitting completes the part. What differs per part is
  how many attempts and whether the student sees the marking.
- **Part A (2.7.1, 40 pts) is ONE attempt.** The correct answers and explanations are released to that
  student immediately afterwards, fetched from the server — never shipped in the page, because the
  key in the page would hand anyone who opens View Source a perfect 40 before their one attempt.
  They get the marking; they do not get a second go. A quiz opts in with `quiz.revealAfterSubmit`,
  which only 2.7.1 sets — `summative` alone does not, because two module quizzes and the Ch 1 and
  Ch 3 tests are `summative` too, and showing marking is not their policy.
- **Part A2 (2.7.2, 10 pts) and Part D2 (2.7.5, 20 pts) are THREE attempts, with the AI's
  feedback shown after each one** — per-question verdicts, marks, summary and hints. The
  **best** attempt is the mark that counts; a later weaker draft cannot lower it. No Run button
  on D2, unchanged: you cannot run the code before you submit it.
- **An attempt the grader could not complete is free.** A row recorded because Ollama was
  unreachable does not spend one of the three, so an outage never costs a student a try.
- **Part C (2.7.3) and Part D1 (2.7.4) allow unlimited attempts** — repairing and redrawing ARE
  the skills. Student sees only "which symptom remains."
- **Attempts made before 2026-09-30 21:49 are grandfathered.** 2.7 opened that morning and
  students sat it on the day the cap was decided; there is no way to un-submit an attempt
  already made, so those students keep what they had and the cap applies from here on.
- **Reading a revised part:** the student may have sent up to three papers. Treat the **highest**
  as the mark and read the first one too — where the class actually was is on the earlier
  submission, not on the polished final one.
- **`check-summative-parts.mjs`** must pass before the day (enforces `summative: true` on all five parts).
- **No retakes** means no second *sitting* on a later day, not one try. Score on Sep 30 is
  final. Absence = teacher judgement.
- **Aeries category:** **Individual Assessments (50%)** per syllabus. 1.7's "15% Individual Chapter Tests" line is wrong — fix it to match syllabus.
