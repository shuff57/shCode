## One inconsistency to note

6.9 Grids (3.9 h), 6.15 Camera (4.3 h) and 6.16 States (5.0 h) are concept modules of 8-10 days
sitting in a unit where a module is one day. Splitting them the way the games were split would
add 20+ modules to unit 6 for no change in time. I would leave them whole and treat the day-module
rule as a rule for **games**, which is what it was written for.
