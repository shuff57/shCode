# Module layout, 2026-11-04 — build everything, skip on the fly

Every game is a chain of day-modules. **Nothing is cut**: Pocket sits in unit 6, and Save &
Load, Joints and Climb are all in the layout, marked `OPTIONAL` so a teacher can pass over them
in a short year without touching the plan.

Hours are authored `estimateMins` plus 10 per sub-build. A day-module is 40-55 minutes.

**What OPTIONAL means in practice:** the module exists, is authored and gated, has no due date
and no open-date row, and simply is not assigned. Nothing in the app needs to change to skip it
— a module with no row is open and ungraded.

Q2's own plan is **23 meetings, ~40 contact hours**. So even with every optional module skipped
the block is about 1.9× the meeting time; the gap is structural, and the two levers that do not
change the layout are:

- **Challenges and playtest tails are already optional extensions** (16 Challenges lessons, 7.5 h).
- **Homework split.** Readings, plans, playtests and reflections are ~20% of the block and do
  not need a meeting. That is the 15-20 h the plan is short.

## Why build all of it anyway

A module that exists and is not assigned costs nothing at runtime: no row, no due date, no lock,
no grade. A module that does not exist costs a build later. Since the layout is a chain of small
modules, skipping one is a decision the teacher makes in the schedule, not a rebuild — and the
student's file carries forward, so a skipped module only means the *next* one's starter is the
last one they actually did.
