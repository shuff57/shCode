# Module layout, 2026-11-04 — build everything, skip on the fly

Every game is a chain of day-modules. **Nothing is deleted.** Save & Load, Camera, Joints and
the Climb arc are marked `OPTIONAL`, and Breakout has moved into unit 7 as the second
build-plainly-then-refactor game beside Defender.

`CORE` is the path a short year can follow end to end; `OPTIONAL` modules exist, are authored and
gated, but carry no due date and no open-date row until a teacher assigns them. Skipping one costs
nothing at runtime and the student's file carries forward.

Hours are authored `estimateMins` plus 10 per sub-build. A day-module is 40-55 minutes.

## Two structural notes

- **Breakout moved as a whole arc, not just its refactor.** Refactoring code the student never
  wrote would be a lie, so the functional Breakout (7.13-7.17) went with it and unit 7 now has
  the same shape twice: build it plainly, then rebuild it with objects. Defender and Breakout
  differ enough — waves and spawning versus a grid and a ball — that the second is transfer,
  not repetition.
- **Grids stays in unit 6 (6.14) and is applied in unit 7's Breakout.** It is the one concept
  whose game now lives in the next unit; the module says so, and 7.15 cites 6.14.
