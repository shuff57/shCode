### Problem 1: 42 → binary

Repeated division:

| Step | Value | ÷ 2 | Quotient | Remainder |
|------|-------|-----|----------|-----------|
| 1 | 42 | ÷ 2 | 21 | **0** |
| 2 | 21 | ÷ 2 | 10 | **1** |
| 3 | 10 | ÷ 2 | 5  | **0** |
| 4 | 5  | ÷ 2 | 2  | **1** |
| 5 | 2  | ÷ 2 | 1  | **0** |
| 6 | 1  | ÷ 2 | 0  | **1** |

Read remainders bottom-to-top: **101010**

Verify: 32 + 8 + 2 = 42. ✓

---

### Problem 2: 117 → binary

Repeated division:

| Step | Value | ÷ 2 | Quotient | Remainder |
|------|-------|-----|----------|-----------|
| 1 | 117 | ÷ 2 | 58 | **1** |
| 2 | 58  | ÷ 2 | 29 | **0** |
| 3 | 29  | ÷ 2 | 14 | **1** |
| 4 | 14  | ÷ 2 | 7  | **0** |
| 5 | 7   | ÷ 2 | 3  | **1** |
| 6 | 3   | ÷ 2 | 1  | **1** |
| 7 | 1   | ÷ 2 | 0  | **1** |

Read remainders bottom-to-top: **1110101**

Verify: 64 + 32 + 16 + 4 + 1 = 117. ✓

---

### Problem 3: 255 → binary

Repeated division:

| Step | Value | ÷ 2 | Quotient | Remainder |
|------|-------|-----|----------|-----------|
| 1 | 255 | ÷ 2 | 127 | **1** |
| 2 | 127 | ÷ 2 | 63  | **1** |
| 3 | 63  | ÷ 2 | 31  | **1** |
| 4 | 31  | ÷ 2 | 15  | **1** |
| 5 | 15  | ÷ 2 | 7   | **1** |
| 6 | 7   | ÷ 2 | 3   | **1** |
| 7 | 3   | ÷ 2 | 1   | **1** |
| 8 | 1   | ÷ 2 | 0   | **1** |

Read remainders bottom-to-top: **11111111**

Verify: 128 + 64 + 32 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 = 255. ✓

255 is a special value in computing: it is the maximum value of an 8-bit (1-byte) unsigned integer, and it appears constantly in color values (RGB uses 0–255 for each channel).
