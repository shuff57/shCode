# Module 2.4: Loop Control and Nested Loops

**Quarter 1 · Weeks 6–7 · Environment: browser DevTools console**

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3**.

## Module Goal
Students can choose between `for`/`while`/`do...while`, control a loop's flow with `break` and `continue`, diagnose an infinite loop, and write and trace a nested loop.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
Module 2.1 (Conditionals), 2.2 (Algorithms and Loops), 2.3 (The switch Statement) complete. No functions, arrays, or try/catch: those are §3.1, §3.3, and §2.5 respectively.

## Post-requisite snapshot
Students can write any of the three loop forms, control their flow with `break`/`continue`, and reason about nested-loop cost and scope: the last console-track prerequisite before try/catch (§2.5) and functions (§3.1).
