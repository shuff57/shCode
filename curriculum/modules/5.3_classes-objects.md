# Module 5.3: Object-Oriented Programming

**Quarter 2 · Week 12 · ~3.5 contact hours**
**Environment:** moSHion in-app editor.

## SLO Focus
- **SLO 2 primary artifact**: A12.2 written OOP-vs-procedural comparison
- This unit is the dual-enrollment anchor for structured-programming documentation

## Unit Goal
Students formally understand what a class is, after two weeks of quietly using `new Sprite(...)`. They can define their own class, instantiate it, and articulate why OOP is more than "just another way to organize code." A12.2 written comparison becomes the teacher's SLO 2 evidence.

## Inter-module bridges
- **Unit 2.1 → 2.2.1:** Two weeks of using `new Sprite(x, y, w, h)` without explanation pays off: the reveal lands because students have muscle memory.
- **2.2.1 → Unit 2.3:** Groups (W13) are a class: students can now inspect `Group`'s methods knowing what a method means.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
- Comfortable with `new Sprite()`, `sprite.color = ...`, and dot access in general.
- Has seen functions (Q1 W6) and understands parameter passing.

## Post-requisite snapshot
- Can write a class with constructor + methods from scratch.
- Can explain what `this` refers to in a method.
- Has written a 1-page comparison of procedural vs OOP (A12.2: retained for SLO 2 evidence).

## Unit-level assessments
No unit-closing quiz: Midterm 3 at W14 covers Units 2.1–2.3.
