# Module 6.11: Sound and Music

**Quarter 2 · Week 18 · after §6.10 Breakout**

## SLO Focus
- **SLO 3:** the module's artifact is a small game (Star catch) with sound effects, background music that follows its screens, and a sound options screen that remembers its settings.
- Reinforces **SLO 2:** one transition function owns the music, the way 6.6 made one variable own the screen.

## Module Goal
Students add sound to a small game one idea at a time. A sound is a file loaded once and played where its event happens once; `loop()` keeps one copy of a tune going; `.volume`, `.rate` and `masterVolume()` set how it sounds; a random rate or a random pick from a list keeps a sound from wearing out; the music changes when the screen changes, in `goTo(next)`; and sound settings are saved with `storeItem` when they change and loaded with `getItem` at the start. The new ideas are play versus loop, the browser's no-sound-before-a-gesture rule, a sound played on an edge (`kb.presses`, a catch), and a saved 0 that `||` would throw away.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
§6.10 complete. Also: `loadSound`, `.play()`, `.volume`, `.rate` and `masterVolume()` met in Defender's sound lab (5.11.9); `kb.presses` against `kb.pressing` (5.1.13, 6.3.3); `overlaps()` is true on every frame two sprites touch (5.9.5); `random(min, max)` and a random position in a list (5.2.1); the state variable and a function called on every change of state (6.6.7, 6.6.24); `storeItem` and `getItem`, which gives back the type saved or `null` (6.5.3, 6.5.5, 6.5.6).

## Post-requisite snapshot
Can load a sound once and play it on an event exactly once, loop a tune under a game and stop it, set a sound's volume and rate and every sound's level at once, vary a sound with a random rate or a random pick, change music in one transition function, and save and load sound settings so a saved 0 survives. Knows that a browser may make no sound until the player clicks or presses a key.

- **A concept module, not a build:** no written plan, no playtest, nothing AI-graded. The labs add one sound idea each to the same small game.
- **The course has two sound files**, `/moshion/assets/coin.wav` and `/moshion/assets/music.wav`. Variety comes from `.rate`, `.volume` and loading one file into more than one variable. **Both files are silent today** (every sample is 0; SOUND-SPEC.md section 0): the labs run and grade correctly but make no audible sound until the two files are replaced with real audio.
- **One tune for every screen, its `.rate` set per screen,** so the music follows the screen without a second file, and a `loop()` waiting for its download cannot start a second tune.
- **Settings use the keys `soundLevel` and `soundMuted`,** not `level`, so they cannot meet a game's own saved level. 6.11.10's game saves under `mySoundLevel` and `mySoundMuted`, so a mute left on in 6.11.8 does not start the challenges silent.
- **Words on screen go in `drawTop()`**, the convention 5.4 set.

### The one idea this module is really about
*A sound belongs to an event, not to a frame.* `draw()` runs 60 times a second, `kb.pressing` and `overlaps()` stay true for many frames, and every `play()` is a new copy. So each sound goes where its event happens exactly once: the frame a key goes down, the `if` that catches a star and moves it away, the one function every change of screen goes through.
