# Module 2.3 — The Switch Statement

**Quarter 1 · Week 6 (Wed Sep 16, 2026) · Environment: browser DevTools console**

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3**.

## Module Goal
Students can write a `switch` statement with `case`/`default`/`break`, explain
and avoid the fall-through and strict-equality traps, group cases that share
an outcome, and choose correctly between `switch` and an `if...else` chain.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
Module 2.1 (Conditionals) and Module 2.2 (Algorithms and Loops) complete.
`break` is taught here as a `switch`-exit keyword only — **not** as loop
control, which is Module 2.4's job. No try/catch, no functions, no arrays.

## Post-requisite snapshot
Students can convert between if-chains and switch statements, predict and fix
fall-through bugs, and know why `switch` never matches a value of the wrong
type. Module 2.4 (Loop Control and Nested Loops) reuses `break`, this time as
a loop-exit — flag the difference explicitly when it's re-taught there.

## What this build added
Rebuilt the module from 5 lessons (a thin slice of book §2.3's six
subsections and 1,024 lines) to 31, using the two-pass method: Pass A mapped
every book element in §2.3.1–§2.3.6 to at least one lesson; Pass B added the
worked examples, predict-then-run labs, and bug-fix drills the book doesn't
supply but the lesson/lab/challenge cycle needs. Highlights:

- A dedicated reading + worked example + two labs for fall-through — the
  book's own #1 flagged bug — instead of one paragraph.
- The two book-given fixes for the strict-equality dead-case bug (match the
  case type vs. convert with `Number()`) split into their own worked
  examples, since they're genuinely different techniques.
- Grouping cases gets a reading, two worked examples (weekend/weekday, then
  the denser days-in-month), and a lab, rather than one combined lesson.
- `2-3-29-a2-3-1-print-settings-advisor` is the real **A2.3.1** artifact
  from `curriculum-plan.md` §2.3 (filament-type switch, 4 cases + default,
  temperature + speed per case, fall-through comment). The pre-existing
  `2-3-4-lab-convert-to-switch` was NOT A2.3.1 — it's kept as ungraded
  practice ahead of the real assignment, renumbered to `2.3.29`.
- `2-3-30-challenges` follows the console-track challenge pattern
  (`type: "assignment"`, `difficulty: "beginner"`, `preview: "console"`) per
  the established inconsistency documented in
  `lab-assignment-conventions.md` §7.7, not the shplay-challenge pattern.
