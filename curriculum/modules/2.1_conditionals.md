# Module 2.1: Conditionals

**Quarter 1 · Week 5 · Environment: browser DevTools console**
**Meets Tue Sep 08 + Thu Sep 10, 2026 (2 meetings, book §2.1 Conditionals, 1329 lines: largest section in the book).**

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3**: students implement their first structured programs with branching.

## Module Goal
Students can make decisions in code: `if` / `else if` / `else`, truthy/falsy conversion, the conditional (ternary) operator `?`, comparison operators (`===` vs `==`), and logical operators (`&& || !`).

## Pre-requisite snapshot
Unit 1 complete: students can declare variables, use the four primitive types, and write expressions.

## Post-requisite snapshot
Students can branch with `if`/`else if`/`else`, pick values with `?`, and combine conditions with logical operators. Ready for §2.2 Algorithms and Loops.
