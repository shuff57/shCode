# Module 2.1 — Conditionals

**Quarter 1 · Week 4 · Environment: browser DevTools console**

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3** — students implement their first structured programs with branching.

## Module Goal
Students can make decisions in code: `if` / `else if` / `else`, comparison operators (`===` vs `==`), and logical operators (`&& || !`).

## Pre-requisite snapshot
Unit 1 complete — students can declare variables, use the four primitive types, and write expressions.

## Post-requisite snapshot
Students can branch with conditionals and combine conditions with logical operators.
