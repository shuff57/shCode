# Module 6.10: Breakout

**Quarter 2 · Week 18 · after §6.14 Grids and Tile Maps**

## SLO Focus
- **SLO 3:** the module's artifact is a playable Breakout with a title screen, three lives and a wall read from text.
- Reinforces **SLO 4:** the wall is a grid kept as data, and the ball's hit test is an algorithm over it.

## Module Goal
Students build Breakout on top of the brick wall from 6.14.11. A ball they move by hand bounces off three walls and a paddle that steers it; the wall is read from rows of text into an array of arrays; the ball asks `hitAt(x, y)` about the four points on its edge, not its centre; the game is won or lost on bricks left and lives; and a title screen lets the player pick the mouse or the keys. The new ideas are a speed held in two variables and flipped to bounce, the radius between a sprite's centre and its edge, and steering by where the ball lands.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
§6.14 complete: a grid of 1s and 0s built with two loops, drawn with `rect`, and `hitAt(x, y)` with the `TOP` offset and a bounds check (6.14.9-6.9.11); a map read as text (6.14.2, 6.14.5). Also: sprites moved by hand and `overlaps()` from Defender (7.5.1), the state variable and key-driven transitions (6.11.7, 6.11.16), `mouse.x` (6.12.3).

## Post-requisite snapshot
Can move a sprite by two speed variables and bounce it by flipping a sign, keep a sprite on screen by its half-width, test a sprite's edges against a grid, end a game on a count, and choose a mode on a title screen. Knows that `prompt()` works in a sketch, gives back a string, and freezes the game.

## Note
**Not a book section.** 13 lessons (`7.13.1`-`7.17.3`) in folders `6-10-breakout-*`, placed after the grids module as decided on 2026-10-31 (`codehs-review/PLACEMENT.md`, decision 7). Spec: `codehs-review/BREAKOUT-SPEC.md`.

- **The ball is moved by code, not physics.** `collider = 'none'` and two variables, `vx` and `vy`, as Defender moved its shots. A physics bounce changes speed and spin, and the bricks are not sprites, so there is nothing for a physics ball to hit.
- **The bricks are `rect`s, not sprites, on purpose,** as in 6.14.11: the grid is the data and the picture is drawn from it.
- **The paddle bounce flips only while the ball is coming down** (`vy > 0`), because `overlaps()` is true on every frame they touch.
- **Words on screen go in `drawTop()`**, the convention 5.4 set.

### The one idea this module is really about
*A sprite has edges, and a grid is asked about points.* `hitAt(x, y)` from 6.14.11 knows nothing about balls; it answers for one pixel. The ball is `R` wide in every direction from its centre, so asking about the centre is asking too late and too narrowly. Asking about the four points on its edge, and flipping `vy` for a brick above or below and `vx` for one beside, is the whole of Breakout's brick collision.
