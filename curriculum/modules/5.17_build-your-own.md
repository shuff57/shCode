# Module 5.17: Build your own

**One day (and homework).** The unit 5 capstone: the student designs and builds a small game of
their own, checked against five requirements drawn only from this unit.

## The one idea

Everything in this unit was our game. This one is theirs: a game they can describe in two
sentences, that is not one of the four they already built, and that uses the mechanics the unit
taught without being told which.
