# Unit 2.6 — Advanced Mechanics

**Quarter 2 · Week 17 · ~3.5 contact hours**
**Environment:** q5play in-app editor.

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3** (implement + debug a complex interactive system)
- Final content week before the capstone

## Unit Goal
Students experiment with q5play's most expressive mechanics: joints (distance/hinge/slider), mouse-based drag-and-release (slingshot), and multi-player input. Most students won't master joints fully — aim for "understand enough to use in capstone." This week also serves as the final wrap-up day for any Unit 2.3–2.5 content that ran long.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
- Classes (2.2), groups (2.3), state machines (2.5) — everything needed to build a complete game.
- Capstone design-doc homework assigned at end of W17.

## Post-requisite snapshot
- Can create at least one joint (distance, hinge, or slider) between two sprites.
- Has implemented a drag-and-release interaction (slingshot pattern).
- Has shipped a two-player game with separate input schemes.
- Has a 1–2 page capstone design doc in hand for W18 Monday.

None at unit close — capstone (A18.1) begins next week.
