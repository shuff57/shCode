# Module 6.3 — Physics Applications

**Quarter 2 · Week 14 · ~3.5 contact hours**
**Environment:** moSHion in-app editor.

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3** (design/implement/test)

## Module Goal
Students apply groups and edge-triggered input to real physics scenarios: jump feel, ground detection, and pendulums — consolidating W13 skills through challenges that preview what their capstone could look like.

## Inter-module bridges
- **6.1 → 6.3:** Groups (W13) are used to build level geometry in W14 challenges (platforms, obstacles).
- **6.3 → 6.4:** Animation & Camera (W15) assumes students can already handle groups of sprites — polish adds on top.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
- Can create a Group, spawn sprites into it, detect overlaps, and despawn safely (Module 6.1).

## Post-requisite snapshot
- Understands edge-triggered vs level-triggered input (`kb.presses` vs `kb.pressing`).
- Has shipped at least one complete playable challenge (Space Jumper or Car on a Ramp).
