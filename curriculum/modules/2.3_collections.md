# Unit 2.3 — Collections and Physics Applications

**Quarter 2 · Weeks 13–14 · ~7 contact hours**
**Environment:** q5play in-app editor.

## SLO Focus
- Reinforces **SLO 3** (design/implement/test)
- Reinforces **SLO 4** (collision detection + spawn logic are algorithms)
- Reinforces Q1 Arrays topic in a motivating new context

## Unit Goal
Students level up from "one sprite" to "many sprites managed as a group." They understand collision detection as an algorithm, handle spawn/despawn logic safely, and consolidate W10–W12 skills through two challenges that preview what their capstone could look like.

## Inter-module bridges
- **2.3.1 → 2.3.2:** Groups (W13) are used to build level geometry in W14 challenges (platforms, obstacles).
- **Unit 2.3 → Unit 2.4:** Animation & Camera (W15) assumes students can already handle groups of sprites — polish adds on top.

## Pre-requisite snapshot
- Comfortable with classes and `new Sprite()` from Unit 2.2.
- Remembers Q1 arrays (push, forEach, iteration).
- Can write a conditional.

## Post-requisite snapshot
- Can create a Group, spawn sprites into it, detect overlaps, and despawn safely.
- Understands edge-triggered vs level-triggered input (`kb.presses` vs `kb.pressing`).
- Has shipped at least one complete playable challenge (Asteroid Field, Space Jumper, or Car on a Ramp).

## Unit-level assessments
- **Semester 1 Midterm 3 (W14):** covers Units 2.1–2.3 (q5play foundations, physics, OOP, groups/overlaps, physics applications).
