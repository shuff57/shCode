# Unit 3.1 — Coordinates & Transforms

**Quarter 3 · Weeks 1–2 · ~6 contact hours**
**Environment:** shPlay in-app editor (three.js facade, `preview: 'shplay'`).

## Theme

"You are now in three dimensions." This module establishes the 3D coordinate system, position, rotation, scale, radians, color, background, and frameCount-driven animation — using **only `Cube`** as the demonstration shape. All other primitives (Sphere, Plane, Cone, Cylinder, Torus) wait for Module 3.2. The constraint is intentional: integration should be about transforms, not shape variety.

## Module Project: Spinning Sculpture

Two stacked Cubes rotating independently on different axes with frameCount-modulated speeds and per-axis color cycling. The student demonstrates mastery of position, rotation (all three axes), scale, radians, color cycling, and time-based animation — plus the `parent()` call to attach the second cube.

## Key constraint

**Module 3.1 uses only `Cube` as the 3D shape.** `Sphere`, `Plane`, `Cone`, `Cylinder`, and `Torus` are introduced in Module 3.2. This isolation ensures students master transforms before they encounter shape variety.

## New concept introduced late in module

`parent(child, parentObj)` is first used in lesson `3-1-b4-lab-stack-cube`. No lesson before that folder should use `parent()`.

## SLO Focus

- Establishes the 3D mental model before any game logic
- Applies the setup()/draw() lifecycle (carried from Unit 2) in a 3D context
- Introduces Euler rotation and the radians helper as the primary new vocabulary

## Concepts taught (new in Unit 3)

1. Bridge from q5play 2D to shPlay 3D — what stays, what changes
2. X axis — positive X = right
3. Y axis — positive Y = UP (key mental flip from screen coordinates)
4. Z axis — positive Z toward viewer, negative Z into scene
5. Right-hand rule and coordinate system handedness
6. The world origin (0,0,0) — default camera at (0,3,7) looking at origin
7. `.position` is a Vector3 with `.x`, `.y`, `.z`
8. Mutating `.position.x` (and .y, .z) in draw() for motion
9. `.rotation` is an Euler with `.x`, `.y`, `.z` in radians
10. What a radian is; `radians(90) = PI/2`; `radians(deg)` helper
11. `.scale.x/y/z` per-axis stretch; `.size` uniform shorthand
12. `background(color)` — clears scene each frame (call in draw())
13. `.color` setter — named colors and hex strings
14. `frameCount` drives steady time-based rotation
15. `parent(child, parentObj)` — child inherits parent's world transform

## Inter-module bridges

- **3.1 → 3.2:** By end of 3.1, students have mastered transforms on a single shape. Module 3.2 keeps all transform knowledge and adds Sphere, Plane, Cone, Cylinder, and Torus — the other five primitives — plus `random()` and multi-shape composition (Solar System project).
- **Unit 2 → 3.1:** `setup()`/`draw()` lifecycle, `kb.pressing()`, and `.color` are identical. Students who completed Unit 2 need zero relearning of these; the bridge reading (3-1-2) makes the mapping explicit.
