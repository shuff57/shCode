## Teacher recording notes

If you record V6 and V7 yourself, aim for:

- **V6 — DevTools Console tour (2–3 min):** Record your screen with DevTools open. Open a blank tab, press `Ctrl+Shift+J`, click the Console tab. Type `console.log("Hello")`, press Enter, point at the output. Keep it calm and slow — this is the first time students see this tool.

- **V7 — SDLC 5-phase overview (under 10 min):** Whiteboard or slides walkthrough. Name each phase, draw the cycle, and use one real app (Instagram, Spotify, Roblox) to make each phase concrete. Students use this video to prep for A1.1.
