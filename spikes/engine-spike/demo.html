teaching-engine spike

Reimplements the q5play-style API your curriculum already teaches (MIT, ours). Physics runs on real Box2D via planck.js (MIT).
Demo echoes A10.1 + physics, a Group/overlap, a HingeJoint (W17), edge input, and camera.

  • Player (pink square): WASD to move, kb.pressing()
  • Auto sprite (cyan circle): orbits using frameCount
  • Ground (grey): static, physics; ball (orange): falls, bounces
  • Pendulum (gold): real Box2D HingeJoint swinging under gravity (W17 proof)
  • Collectibles (green Group): overlap to collect, overlaps() + remove()
  • Space: edge-triggered jump (kb.presses())
  • Camera: follows the player when they reach the right half
  • High score: storeItem/getItem (localStorage)
