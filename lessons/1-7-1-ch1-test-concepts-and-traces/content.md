## Chapter 1 Test — Part 1 of 5: Concepts and Traces

**Worth 40 of the 100 points on this test.** Eight questions: three about ideas
from the chapter, five where you read a short program and say what it prints.

**This one is a test, so it does not behave like the quizzes.**

- **You submit once.** When you press submit the paper locks and does not reopen.
- **Nothing is marked on screen.** No green, no red, no notes explaining the
  answer. Your teacher hands the score back.
- **Submitting is what unlocks Part 2**, not passing. Answer everything, submit,
  and move on.

You can change any answer as many times as you like *before* you submit.

### On the tracing questions

Read the code and work out what it prints. Every one of them is a straight run of
statements — no decisions, no loops, nothing Chapter 1 has not taught. Two of
them come out differently from what the numbers look like they should say; that
is the point of asking.

Work them out on paper if it helps. Do not open a console: this is closed-book,
and your teacher is watching for it.

### The papers are not the same

Everyone in the room gets the same eight questions in different words and
different numbers, in a different order. Reading someone else's screen will cost
you the time it takes to notice.
