## Plan before you build

A siege game is five parts: a catapult on the ground, a wall of blocks, a shot you launch,
a rule for what a hit does, and a budget of shots. Answer these four questions in the box.

1. **Parts.** Name the four or five parts of the game.
2. **Remember.** What does the game have to remember between frames? (The shots in the air, the
   blocks left, the shots left.)
3. **First.** What will you build first, so something runs today?
4. **Unsure.** Which part are you least sure how to build?
