## Chapter 1 Individual PA — Part 5 of 5: Write the Steps

**20 points — the largest single item.** Pseudocode for the problem you charted
in Part 4. The same one.

- **Not JavaScript.** Plain English, arranged like code.
- One submission. It locks, and nothing comes back on screen.
- Part 4 is the lesson right before this one. Going back to look at your own
  chart costs you nothing.

### The two rules

- **One instruction per line.** A line doing two things gets split.
- **Indent what sits inside something else.** Moving a line in or out describes
  a different program.

The same shape on a different problem (1.5.21, 1.5.22):

```
get the pizza price
get the number of slices
set cost per slice to price divided by slices
if cost per slice is 2.00 or less
    report that it is a good deal
otherwise
    report that it costs too much
```

### The three problems

- **1. Backpack Check** — number of textbooks, weight of one textbook, the
  student's own weight → what the backpack weighs, against **one tenth of the
  student's weight**
- **2. Screen Time** — minutes used across **seven days** → the daily average,
  against **120 minutes a day**
- **3. Print Job** — number of pages at **8 cents a page** → what the job costs,
  against the **$5.00** left in the account

### What is marked

- Every value the problem gives you is fetched before it is used
- The one value is worked out, in a line that says **how**
- The comparison is asked as a question, and **both** answers are written
- The lines under each answer are indented, and nothing else is
- Plain language throughout, one instruction per line
