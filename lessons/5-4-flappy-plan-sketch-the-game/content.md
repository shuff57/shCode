## Plan before you build

Every game in this unit gets a plan first. A plan is not code. It is a short list that says what the game is made of, so the first thing you type is the right thing.

**Flappy, in one paragraph.** A bird sits at the left of the screen and is always falling. A key tap makes it jump up a little. Pipes with a gap in them slide in from the right, and the bird has to fly through the gaps. Each pipe it gets past adds one to the score. Touching a pipe ends the round, and a key starts it again.

**What a plan lists.** Break it into parts, as you did in the pinball build when you split the table, the score and the ending. For each part ask: does the game have to *remember* something about it from one moment to the next, the way pinball remembered the score? If so, that is a variable, and the plan names it.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. At least four parts of the game.
2. At least two things it must remember.
3. What you would build first, and why.
4. One part you are unsure about.
