## Chapter 1 Test — Part 2 of 5: In Your Own Words

**Worth 10 of the 100 points on this test.** Three short answers. Two or three
sentences each is plenty; nobody is counting words.

**Same rules as Part 1.** One submission, it locks, nothing comes back on screen.
Submitting is what opens Part 3.

Your own everyday words throughout. An answer that repeats a definition without
using it scores lower than a plainer one that shows you know what the idea is
for.

### 1. Umbrella activities

Chapter 1 split the work of building software into framework activities and
**umbrella** activities. What makes an activity an umbrella activity? Name one,
and say what would go wrong on a project that skipped it.

### 2. Read this and say what is wrong with it

```js
// set x
let x = 12.5;
// set y
let y = 3;
// multiply
let z = x * y;
console.log(z);
```

The program runs and prints the right number. Name **two** things that are still
wrong with it, and for each one say what it costs the next person who has to
change this code. One of your two has to be about the comments.

### 3. High and low

Explain the difference between a high-level language and a low-level language in
your own words, then give one reason a team might deliberately choose the
lower-level one even though it is harder to write.
