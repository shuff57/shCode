## Playtest and reflect

A playtest is playing your own work on purpose, to find out what it does, not to win. The labs
checked that your code has the right shape; only playing it shows whether it behaves the way you
meant.

**How to play on purpose.** Play your finished Trail from 6.25.3 (Lab: Trail online) at least
three times. Play once online if you can: with a classmate, or by yourself with the lesson open in
a second browser tab (6.25.1). Then try to break it. Land on a J, an S and a W. Roll when a token
is a few squares from the end. Online, press SPACE when it is not your turn, and press R. Watch
for anything that looks odd: a token that ends up off the board, a player who waits forever, two
boards that do not match.

**Then think backwards.** When something looks odd, ask which part of your code could cause it:
`makePath`, the die, `takeTurn`, `nextTurn`, a letter's `if`, the waiting flag, `online.send` or
`onMove`. Naming the line is the first step of fixing it, even when you are not sure.

**Then choose one change.** One rule or number you would change, such as a longer track, a jump
of 4, a new letter, or three players, and what it would do to the game.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. How many times you played, and how it went.
2. One thing that surprised you or did not work as you expected.
3. Which line or part of your code you think that comes from.
4. One rule or number you would change, and what it would do.
