## Chapter 3 Individual PA — Part 4 of 4: Reflect

**A short write-up, done alone.** The program is written; this is where you say what
happened. It is worth **10 points**, about **7 minutes**, and short answers are fine.

**You get three tries.** Submit, read the feedback on each item, improve and submit again.
Your best try counts. After your third try you can see how it is solved, once your teacher
releases it.

### 1. Three test cases (4 points)

A test case is one run of your program where you **know the right answer before you run
it**. Write three, in a table with these columns:

| What you put in | What you expected | What you got |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the records or numbers you gave it | the answer you worked out by hand first | what your program printed |

Your program makes one yes-or-no decision: whether the total is **over the budget** (Price
Check), **reaches the weekly goal** (Reading Log), or **fits one fill of the watering can**
(Watering Rota). A good set of tests checks **both** answers, so **at least one case must
be over and at least one must be under** (or one fit and one not).

| Your problem | Over / not enough | Under / enough |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price Check, budget 10 | a cart totalling 14: over budget | a cart totalling 7: within budget |
| Reading Log, goal 150 | a week of 180 minutes: reaches the goal | a week of 120: falls short |
| Watering Rota, can 500 ml | a rota needing 620 ml: more than one fill | a rota needing 380 ml: one fill is enough |

Then one line: **did "what you got" match "what you expected" every time?** If not, say what
you changed.
