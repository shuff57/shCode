## Playtest and reflect

A playtest is playing your own game on purpose, to find out what it does, not to win. Programmers do it after every build, because code that passes the labs can still feel wrong.

**How to play on purpose.** Play your finished pinball at least three rounds, and try to lose in a different way each time. Watch for anything that looks odd: a score that jumps by too much, a ball that sticks, a restart that leaves something behind.

**Then think backwards.** When something looks odd, ask which part of the game could cause it: the score, the memory of the last moment, the flag that says whether the round is being played, the restart. Naming the part is the first step of fixing it, even when you are not sure.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. How many rounds you played and how they went.
2. One thing that surprised you or did not work as you expected.
3. Which part of the code you think that comes from.
4. One rule or number you would change, and what it would do.
