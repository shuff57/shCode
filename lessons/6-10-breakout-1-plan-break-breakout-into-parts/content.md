## Plan before you build

Every project in this unit gets a plan first, as the Coin Maze did in 6.14.1 (Plan: Break the Coin Maze into parts). A plan is not code. It is a short list that says what the game is made of, so the first thing you type is the right thing.

**This module in one paragraph.** You will build Breakout. A paddle slides along the bottom, steered by the mouse or the arrow keys. A ball bounces off the left wall, the right wall, the top and the paddle, and falls out at the bottom if you miss it. Above, a wall of coloured bricks waits: the brick wall you built in 6.14.11, written as rows of text this time, and the ball asks your `hitAt(x, y)` whether it has hit a brick. Clear the wall to win; miss three balls and you lose. Last, a title screen lets the player pick how to steer.

**What a plan lists.** Break Breakout into parts, the way the Coin Maze became walls, coins, a goal and levels. For each part ask: does the game have to *remember* something about it from frame to frame? If so, that is a variable, and the plan names it.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. At least four parts of Breakout.
2. At least two things it must remember between frames.
3. What you would build first, and why.
4. One part you are unsure about.
