## Play it, then write

Play three rounds. Each time, try a different aim: a flat shot, a high lob, a shot at the bottom
of the wall.

Answer four things in the box:

1. **Played.** What did you do, and how did it go?
2. **Surprise.** One thing that surprised you.
3. **Cause.** The line in your code that causes it.
4. **Change.** One change you would make, and why.

"The wall falls over on its own" is a good surprise if you can point at the block that moves.
