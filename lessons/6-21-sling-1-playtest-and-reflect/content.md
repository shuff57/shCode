## Play it, then write

A playtest is playing your own work on purpose, to find out what it does, not to win. Play your game from 6.20.1 (Lab: Five shots, win or lose) three times. Each time, try a different aim: a flat shot, a high lob, a shot at the bottom of the wall.

"The wall falls over on its own" is a good surprise if you can point at the block that moves.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. How many times you played, and how it went.
2. One thing that surprised you or did not work as you expected.
3. Which line or part of your code you think that comes from.
4. One rule or number you would change, and what it would do.
