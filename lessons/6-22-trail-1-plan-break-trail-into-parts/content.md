## Plan before you build

Trail is a race game for two players on one board. The board is a path of squares that winds
back and forth across the screen. Each player has a token. On your turn you roll a die and move
that many squares. Some squares do something when you land on them: one sends you ahead, one
sends you back, one makes you miss a turn. The first token to reach the last square wins.

Over five days you will build all of it, and on day four you will play it against a classmate
on another computer. Answer these four questions in the box.

1. **Parts.** Name the four or five parts of the game.
2. **Remember.** What does the game have to remember between frames? (The squares, where each
   token is, whose turn it is, who has won.)
3. **First.** What will you build first, so something runs today?
4. **Unsure.** Which part are you least sure how to build?
