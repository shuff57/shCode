# Book Order Total

## What is it?

This program works out what a book order costs, adding sales tax to the price of the copies.

## How to run?

Press Run and the book title, the price before tax, and the final total appear in the console.

## Know first?

The tax rate is the TAX_RATE line at the top of script.js, and it is the one number you will have to change when the rate goes up.
