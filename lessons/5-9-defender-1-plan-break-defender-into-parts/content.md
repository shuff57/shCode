## Plan before you build

Every game in this unit gets a plan first. A plan is not code. It is a short list that says what the game is made of, so the first thing you type is the right thing.

**Defender, in one paragraph.** A ship slides left and right along the bottom of a 400 x 400 canvas, above a grey base strip. Holding Space fires yellow shots straight up, at most one every 12 frames. Enemies fall from the top in waves: crimson ones take one shot (worth 10), every third one is orange and takes three (worth 30). An enemy that reaches the base costs the base 1 of its 3 hp; at 0 the round is over, and R starts a new one. Each wave has two more enemies than its number and falls a little faster.

You will build it in three parts: 7.4 the ship and its shots against a single drone, 7.5 many enemies and waves, 7.6 the same game rebuilt with classes.

**What a plan lists.** Break it into parts, as you did for Snake in 5.13.1 (Plan: Break Snake into parts), where the snake's body, the food and the steering each became a part of their own. For each part ask: does the game have to *remember* something about it from frame to frame? If so, that is a variable, and the plan names it.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. At least four parts of the game.
2. At least two things it must remember between frames.
3. What you would build first, and why.
4. One part you are unsure about.
