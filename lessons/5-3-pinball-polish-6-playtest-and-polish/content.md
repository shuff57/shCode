## Playtest and polish

The playtest in 5.9.1 hunted for bugs: a score that jumped, a ball that stuck, a restart that left something behind. A polish pass asks a different question. It is not about bugs. It is about whether a new player understands what is happening **without being told**.

**How to playtest for polish.** Play your table at least three rounds. Then, if you can, hand it to someone else for one round and say nothing at all: no "press R", no "hold the arrows". Watch them. The moments where they stop, frown or ask you something are the places your game still needs polish.

**What to watch for:**

- Can they tell what a bumper is worth before they hit it?
- Do they notice what a hit gave them, or does the popup go by too fast or too slow?
- Do they find the nudge on their own, and does it feel like a nudge or a launch?
- Is there a moment where nothing seems to be happening and they do not know what to do?

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. How many rounds you played, or watched, and how they went.
2. One thing that still felt unfinished or rough, or a moment where a player hesitated.
3. One change from this polish arc (the labels, the popups, the nudge, the ball's mass) and what it changed for a player.
4. One more polish you would add next.
