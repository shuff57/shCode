## Playtest and reflect

A playtest is playing your own game on purpose, to find out what it does, not to win. Programmers do it after every build, because the code that passes the labs can still feel wrong.

**How to play on purpose.** Play at least three rounds, and lose in a different way each time: off a wall, into your own body, and by running around until you are very long. Watch for anything that looks odd, such as the food appearing somewhere strange, the speed feeling off, or the snake growing at the wrong moment.

**Then think backwards.** When something looks odd, ask which part of the game could cause it: the beat, the head and tail swap, the search, the food placement. Naming the part is the first step of fixing it, even when you are not sure.

Write your response in the box below. Short sentences or a numbered list both work. Aim to cover:

1. How many rounds you played and how they went.
2. One thing that surprised you or did not work as you expected.
3. Which part of the code you think that comes from.
4. One rule or number you would change, and what it would do.
